How to add staff photos to the Chulo Cottage website

1. Save each photo as a JPG (a portrait/upright photo looks best).
2. Name them exactly:
     staff-1.jpg   Shilpa Basnet, Founder & Managing Director
     staff-2.jpg   Head Chef
     staff-3.jpg   Barista
     staff-4.jpg   Guest Host
3. Put them in the "img" folder next to index.html and upload.

To change names or roles, open index.html, search for "var STAFF" and edit the
name and role text. Add or remove lines to show more or fewer people
(for example staff-5.jpg). Until a photo is added, the card shows the person's initials.
